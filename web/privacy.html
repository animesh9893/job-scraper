Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website and how it is used.

No account needed

You can browse every job on this site without signing up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell personal information.

Job listings

The job listings and salary figures shown here come from publicly available sources such as LinkedIn and Glassdoor. They describe jobs and companies, not visitors to this site. If a listing contains information about you that you want removed, email animesh.shrivatri2002@gmail.com.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google will ask for your consent before using cookies for personalised ads. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics (Umami)

We use Umami to count visits and see which pages are popular. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. It records anonymous data such as the page visited, the referring site, your browser, device type and country, and it does not track you across other websites. See Umami’s privacy policy.

Technical data

Our hosting provider (Vercel) and our database provider (Supabase) may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and secure the service. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Telegram job alerts

If you choose to use our Telegram bot, we store your Telegram chat ID, your Telegram username and the keywords and locations you set, so we can send you matching jobs. Send /stop to the bot to stop alerts, or email us to have your data deleted.

Links to other sites

Job links take you to LinkedIn or other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

This site is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

To ask about, correct or delete any information we hold about you, email animesh.shrivatri2002@gmail.com. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
